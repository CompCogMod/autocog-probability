# howe2022 — Howe, Perfors et al. (2022, JDM): base rate neglect & conservatism with full distributions

Source: `data/raw/howe2022/repo/Experiment{1,2,3}_Andy.csv` (raw, one row per elicited distribution; the
`datafiles/*_adj.csv` files are derived: excluded people dropped and +0.01 smoothing). Converter:
`scripts/convert_data/howe2022.py`. 1202 rows, 1052 subjects, 62 items.

## Design
A bag of chips has unknown proportion red θ ∈ {0, .1, …, 1}. Subjects saw chips drawn (red/blue) and spread
probability over the 11 θ values (weights sum to 1).
- **exp1** (452 Ss): `five` (saw 4R1B), `unlimited` (drew as many as wanted), `all` (prior → after first five →
  after unlimited draws; 3 elicitations). No prior given.
- **exp2** (300 Ss): given prior histogram, `uniform` or `peaked` ([0,0,0,0,0,.08,.08,.2,.6,.04,0]); saw 4R1B.
- **exp3** (300 Ss): symmetric humped prior (vector from data_creation_exp3.Rmd); `given` (shown histogram) vs
  `estimated` (learned it by experience, then reported own prior); saw 4R1B.

## Columns
- One row per posterior distribution; elicited priors are attached to their posterior rows (not separate rows).
- hypothesis H = "next chip is red", so every P(H) is a distribution **mean**:
  `posterior_reported` = mean of elicited posterior; `prior_reported` = mean of elicited prior (exp1 all, exp3
  estimated); `prior_objective` = mean of design prior (exp1: uniform assumed, as in the authors' Bayes reference).
- `p_signal_h`/`p_signal_not_h`: predictive likelihoods of the ordered chip sequence given next-red/next-blue
  under the design prior, so `posterior_bayes` = exact Bayesian posterior mean. `posterior_bayes_rep_prior` =
  exact full-distribution Bayes posterior mean under the subject's reported prior (NOT the binary formula with
  `log_lr`, since the predictive LR depends on the prior).
- Evidence is cumulative from the start (exp1 all/unlimited: seenRed/seenBlue include the first five; `round`=1).
- `trial` = elicitation order incl. the prior elicitation. `condition` in exp1 = `<condition>/<section>`.
- item_id = `study:prior:r<red>b<blue>`.
- extra: `dist_bins`, `dist_weights` (raw elicited posterior), `prior_dist_weights`, `objective_prior_weights`,
  `objective_prior_source`, `bayes_post_dist_weights`, `posterior_mode`, `posterior_sd`, `chips_seq` (R/B),
  `chips_seq_complete`, `chipsOrder_raw`, age, gender, botCheck, actuallyEstimated, `excluded_paper`,
  `time_total_sec` (whole session, not per judgment; rt_sec NaN).

## Exclusions
All kept. `excluded_paper` = subject failed botCheck or actuallyEstimated==0 (authors' rule): 90/61/39 subjects
in exp1/2/3 (362/239/261 kept).

## Sanity
Mean reported vs Bayes posterior: overall .632 vs .712 (conservative/regressive to .5). E.g. exp1 five .650 vs .707;
exp2 peaked .685 vs .762, uniform .591 vs .707; exp3 given .570 vs .646, estimated .626 vs .646.

## Caveats
- Binary reduction (mean) loses distribution shape; use `extra.dist_weights` for distribution-level modelling
  (the paper fits β = prior weight vs uniform, γ/δ = red/blue evidence weights to the distributions).
- exp3 `estimated` objective prior assumed equal to the given histogram (the learned environment).
- exp1 has no stated prior; uniform assumed.
- chipsOrder for long sequences is truncated (~15 digits, numeric precision loss) — use seenRed/seenBlue counts;
  exp2 has no chip sequence (always 4R1B, order unknown).
- Elicited weights sometimes sum to 0.9996–1.0004; means are normalised.
