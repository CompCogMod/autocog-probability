# hill2017 — Hill (2017), "Learning Together Slowly: Bayesian Learning About Political Facts", J. Politics

Converter: `scripts/convert_data/hill2017.py` → `data/processed/hill2017.csv.gz` (15,040 rows, 1,385 subjects, 10 item ids).

## Design
- **exp1** (MTurk, N=990; `AnalysisData-long/wide.tab`, `PewWeights.tab`). Each subject plays 3 "contests":
  `contest1`, `contest2` = two partisan political facts (randomly drawn from 6 statements, 3 whose truth favours Democrats,
  3 Republicans), and `selfcon` = ego contest "your IQ-quiz score is in the top half". Per contest: initial belief
  P(statement true) on 0–100 (round 1), then 4 binary TRUE/FALSE signals, each 75% accurate (raw `sig.comb` = ±ln 3),
  belief re-elicited after each. 990 × 3 × 4 = 11,880 posterior rows.
- **exp2** (N=395; `Exper2-DataForStata.tab`). 2 contests: abstract (Doha day length on 8 Jan 2012, "more"/"less" wording)
  and partisan (Obama income; Reagan debt "more than doubled" vs "cut by more than 50%"). The statement wording shown
  was randomised to be true or false. 395 × 2 × 4 = 3,160 posterior rows.
- Empirical signal accuracy vs truth: 75.1% (exp1), 74.7% (exp2) — matches 75%.

## Column derivation
- `round` = raw round − 2 (raw round 1 = initial belief, not a row; it is `prior_reported` of round 0). `trial` = order of
  posterior judgments within subject assuming contest order contest1, contest2, selfcon (exp1) and abstract, partisan (exp2, file order).
  **True presentation order of contests is not in the data** — treat `trial` across contests as uncertain; within-contest order is exact.
- `prior_reported` = subject's previous-round response /100 (own computation; raw `pr.true.prev` kept in `extra`).
- `posterior_reported` = `pr.true`/100 (exp1) or `belieforig`/100 (exp2).
- `hypothesis`: exp1 facts "statement true" (only one wording per fact); exp1 IQ "own IQ-quiz score in top half";
  exp2 "statement true" for the **wording as displayed** (`belieforig`, `signalorig`). Raw truth-oriented recodes
  (`prtrue`, `signalrecoded`) are in `extra`.
- Signals: `p_signal_h`/`p_signal_not_h` = 0.75/0.25 (TRUE) or 0.25/0.75 (FALSE); `n_signals`=1; `log_lr`=±ln 3.
- `prior_objective` / `posterior_bayes`: NaN — no objective prior (factual questions). `posterior_bayes_rep_prior` = previous
  reported belief updated by step LR (step-wise Bayes). The paper's benchmark `perf.bayes.cert`/`perfbayescert` (first-round
  belief + cumulative signals, 0–100) is kept in `extra` (exp2 only has it for the partisan contest).
- `rt_sec` = exp1 `time.submit` (seconds on page, assumed); negative values set NaN (`flag_negative_rt`). None for exp2.
- `condition`: exp1 IQ rows record `iq_question_asked_as_{top,bottom}_half` (`iq.orig.qx.bottom`; data already recoded to
  "top half" by the author — raw-frame response would be 1 − p); exp1 fact rows ''; exp2 `<abstract|partisan>_contest;statement_version=<true|false>`.
- `task_domain`: political_fact; ego (IQ); other (Doha day length — non-political factual, not urn-style abstract).
- `extra`: item truth (`item_truth`; for IQ = quizAboveBelow=="Above"), which party the truth favours and whether it favours
  the subject's party, did.not.update, cum.sig.comb, Pew weight (exp1) / weight (exp2), all wide-file covariates (age, party,
  ideology, quiz score, education, gender, state, voting, favourability, policy items, `lookup.answers`, MTurk experience, …).

## Items
Full statement texts for the 6 exp1 facts (and truth values) come from `99_CallData.R`. Exp2 statement text is truncated to 100
chars in the raw file; Doha and "debt cut by 50%" items carry the truncated text (marked) — threshold values are not recoverable.

## Exclusions
None applied. The paper's main analysis weights to Pew (`pew_weight`, flags for missing/zero weights) and optionally splits by
quiz score; responses of 0/100 are logit-adjusted in the paper (6.4% of rows are exactly 0 or 1 here — left as is).
`lookup.answers` (self-reported looking up answers) is kept as a covariate for optional filtering.

## Sanity checks
- 990 + 395 subjects, 4 posteriors per contest, matching the paper.
- corr(posterior_reported, posterior_bayes_rep_prior) = 0.68 (exp1), 0.65 (exp2); corr with paper benchmark = 0.50.
- Of rows where belief changed, 79% move in the signal's direction; 54% of steps show no change (many at 0/100 extremes).
